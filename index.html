Almond Croissants

A delicate pastry filled with the sweet nutty goodness of almond paste,dusted with powdred sugar,and baked to a gold perfection

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet of puff pastry
  • 1/2 cup of almond paste
  • 1/4 cup of powdered sugar
  • 1 egg(for egg wash)

Intstructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees fahrenheit
  2. Rollout the puff pastry and cut it into triangles
  3. Spread of thin layer of almond paste on each triangle
  4. Roll each triangle from the base to the tip,and curve into a crescent shape
  5. Finally brush with egg wash and sprinkle with powdered sugar,and bake for 15-20 mintues until its golden brown

Sourodugh Bread

A community favorite,boasting crusty exterior and a soft,airy interior,embodying the classic sourfough charm

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of sourdough starter
  • 1 1/2 cups warm water
  • 4 cups of bread flour
  • 1 1/2 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Mix the starter,water,and 3 cups of flour in a large bowl.This should create a sticky dough
  2. Add salt,and remaining flour 1 teaspoon at a time until they are fully incorporated
  3. Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 15 mintues
  4. Once the kneading is done,place the dough in a lightly greased bowl,cover it, and let it rise for about 5 hours
  5. Finally shapee the dough into a loaf,and let it rest for 30 mintues.when you are ready to bake,preheatr your oven to 450 degree farenheit and bake for about 30 mintues until its golden brown

Classic Carrot Cake

An homage to tradition and familial bonds,enriched with the natrual sweetness of carrots and a crunch of walnuts,it holds a special place in my heart as it was my late grandfather's favorite.Every year on his grandfather's birthday,I bake this cake to honor his memory and the bond we shared over the love of baking

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup of vegatable oil
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 cups granted carrots
  • 1 cup crushed pineapple
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degree fahrenheit
  2. Mix together the flour,sugar,baking soda,cinnamon,and salt in large bowl
  3. Stir in the oil and eggs until smooth,fold in the carrots,pineapple,and walnuts,and then our the batter into a greased baking pan and bake for 30-35 minutes
  4. Make sure you allow the cake to cool before frosting with cream cheese frosting

Tips and tricks

  • Measure ingredients accurately
  • allow eggs and butter to reach room temperature before baking
  • rotate the baking tray halfway through the baking time for an even bake
